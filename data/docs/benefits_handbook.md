# Employee Benefits Handbook (Acme Corp)

## Paid time off
Full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid time off (PTO) per year, increasing to 25 days after 5 years of service. Up to 5 unused PTO days can be carried over into the next year. Acme also observes 10 public holidays annually.

## Parental leave
Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks of fully paid leave. Leave may be taken at any time within the first 12 months after birth or adoption.

## Health and retirement
Acme covers 90% of medical premiums for employees and 70% for dependents. The company matches 401(k) contributions dollar for dollar up to 5% of salary, vesting immediately.

## Learning budget
Every employee has an annual learning budget of $1,500 for courses, books and conferences. Unused learning budget does not roll over. Conference travel is paid separately under the travel and expense policy.
