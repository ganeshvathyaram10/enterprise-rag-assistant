# Travel and Expense Policy (Acme Corp)

## Submission deadlines
Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date. Reports submitted after 60 days will not be reimbursed. Receipts are required for every expense over $25.

## Approval limits
Managers can approve expenses up to $2,500. Expenses between $2,500 and $10,000 require director approval. Anything above $10,000 requires CFO approval before the money is spent.

## Travel
Flights must be booked in economy class through the Acme travel portal at least 14 days in advance. Business class is allowed only for flights longer than 8 hours and requires VP approval. Hotel stays are capped at $220 per night in major cities and $160 per night elsewhere.

## Meals
The daily meal allowance during business travel is $75. Alcohol is not reimbursable. Client entertainment meals are capped at $120 per person and require the client name and business purpose on the receipt.

## Reimbursement
Approved expenses are reimbursed by direct deposit within 10 business days.
