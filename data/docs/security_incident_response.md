# Security Incident Response Procedure (Acme Corp)

## Reporting
Any employee who suspects a security incident must report it to the Security Operations Center (SOC) within 1 hour of discovery by emailing security@acme.example or calling the 24/7 hotline. Do not attempt to investigate or delete evidence yourself.

## Severity levels
Severity 1 (critical) means confirmed data breach or ransomware; the response team must be assembled within 15 minutes. Severity 2 (high) means a compromised account or malware on a single machine; response starts within 1 hour. Severity 3 (low) covers phishing emails with no click-through and is handled within 24 hours.

## Containment and notification
The incident commander may isolate affected systems from the network. If personal data of customers is involved, Legal must be notified within 24 hours, and regulators are notified within 72 hours where required by law. Customers are notified only after Legal approval.

## Post-incident review
A blameless post-incident review must be held within 5 business days of resolution of any Severity 1 or Severity 2 incident. The review produces a written report with root cause and action items, owned by the incident commander.
