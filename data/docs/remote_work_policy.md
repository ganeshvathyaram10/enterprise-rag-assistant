# Remote Work Policy (Acme Corp)

## Eligibility
Employees who have completed their 90-day probation period are eligible for hybrid work. Hybrid employees must be in the office at least 3 days per week, on Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday. Fully remote arrangements require written approval from a VP and are reviewed every 12 months.

## Equipment and stipend
Acme provides a laptop and one external monitor to every remote employee. Remote employees receive a one-time home-office stipend of $750, claimable within the first 60 days of approval. A monthly internet reimbursement of up to $50 is available with a receipt submitted through the expense portal.

## Working from abroad
Employees may work from another country for a maximum of 20 days per calendar year. Requests must be submitted to HR at least 30 days in advance because of tax and legal review. Working abroad without approval is a policy violation and may lead to disciplinary action.

## Security requirements
All remote work must use the corporate VPN. Company data may not be stored on personal devices. Public Wi-Fi may only be used together with the VPN.
